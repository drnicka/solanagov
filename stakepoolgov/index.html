Reading the trade-offs

Shrink the full-rate band → turns 1.0× into a flat floor everyone gets; funds the small end.

Lower the mid-band rate → taxes the band where most eligible stake sits (funds ~6pp of redistribution at 0.6×).

Lower the top rate → the single largest funding source; hits the >1.5M bracket.

Ceiling vs stake cap → the stake cap is a single-name rule (only Binance binds, −46%); the pool-share ceiling spreads the top-end cut evenly.

Delegation moved → fixed income loss that the subsidy must offset; watch the Combined Income table — the memo targets −9% to −11% below 1.5M.

Half-life → shorter = cheaper programme, sharper step-down; longer = softer landing, more spend.

Whale spotlight — the top end under the cap

The current 3M effective-stake cap binds at ~4.5M raw stake — only Binance is above that line. Carrying it forward cuts Binance −46% while peers take ~−30%. A pool-share ceiling spreads the load; the ceiling level is the dial.

Combined income (subsidy + delegation) as the move phases in — median validator

Delegation moves 7.5% of the pool per Steward cycle until the target is reached. New subsidy rates apply from cycle 1 while delegation losses ramp — the memo's sequencing keeps the small brackets near-flat for ~6 weeks. Memo target at completion: −9% to −11% for all brackets below 1.5M.
